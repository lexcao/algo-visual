# Binary search visualizer

## Mode

Operate

## Audience

A learner, often reading Chinese first and English second, who came to watch binary search discard half a range.

## Job

Pick a variant, pick a target, step once, and see the bounds and the source line change together.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a working study tool whose first viewport is the array and the next step. It refuses the category's card grid of algorithm names and refuses a costume of any instrument.

OWN-WORLD: Lacquer shell (#1A1410) and bone work surface (#F3EEE6), cinnabar (#8E2A22) used for the midpoint, the span, and the single primary button. Noto Serif SC for the title, Noto Sans SC for the UI, JetBrains Mono only inside the code. Flat cells, hairline rules, one contracting span.

STORY: The visitor reads one Chinese sentence and one English sentence, sees low / mid / high on a real array, and presses 开始 or 下一步. Each step writes a single sentence about what just happened. Reset is the only other action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed lacquer bar with the name and four variant links. Below, a left-aligned serif title, two short sentences, then the number row at the largest scale on the page with a cinnabar span and end ticks under the live range. The status sentence and the primary button sit directly under the row. Code starts at the bottom edge of a short laptop screen, never above the array.

FORM: Assigned grounded candidate 4, the suanpan span: the world lends only type, palette, density, and the contracting measured span. Seed key 407cff13. Layout and controls stay ordinary web controls.

RAISE — Ikeda contrast: cells in range are full ink; discarded cells drop to one quiet tone, with no equal-weight rainbow.

RAISE — Wayfinding next-decision: one status sentence and one filled button.

RAISE — J-card tally: a tabular step count is always visible.

RAISE — Feed scale: the number row is the largest element in the viewport.

RAISE — Catalog index: the index is a divided list of real arrays, not illustrated cards.

SIGNATURE: The span's left and width ease closed (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)) when a bound moves. prefers-reduced-motion cuts that transition.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
