二分查找

在已经排好序的序列里，每次只看中点，然后丢掉不可能包含目标的那一半。选一个变体，点一个数作为目标，再一步一步往下走。

In a sorted sequence, each step looks at the midpoint and discards the half that cannot hold the target. Choose a variant, pick a target, and walk the search one step at a time.

  • 有序数组 Sorted array
  • 含重复 Duplicates
  • 旋转 Rotated
  • 旋转且重复 Rotated, duplicates
